# Status and limitations

## Tested on phones

Nokia 6160 and Motorola V60t:

- Registration, including periodic
- SMS to and from phones
- Calls between two phones, with speech both ways
- Calls set up with ACELP CC1
- SIP calling
- SMS hub (SMPP)

## Not supported

- Authentication and encryption
- Handoff
- Analog service
- More than two calls at a time, or a second call alongside a call between two local phones
- Multiple cells working together
- ACELP CC2, AMR, data calls, DTX
- Supplementary services
